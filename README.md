I’m a data analyst with a Master’s in Data Science and three years of applied data research at **LIMOS, Université Clermont Auvergne**.

My research focused on missing data, probabilistic modelling, and query processing. It shapes how I approach analytics: check data quality, test assumptions, and communicate both findings and limitations.
